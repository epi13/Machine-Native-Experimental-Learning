# Roadmap

## 0.1 — executable method foundation

- canonical identities and append-only ledger;
- investigator contracts;
- experiment lifecycle and governor;
- independent hard-gate evaluator;
- causal attribution and VED proposals;
- provider-neutral integration records;
- schema, reference study, tests, CI, and documentation.

## 0.2 — local investigator harness

- native adapter to `epi13-local-harness`;
- eligible-context retrieval and packing;
- investigator portfolio scheduling;
- explicit read-only and proposal workspaces;
- Git worktree or snapshot-isolated candidate transactions;
- model, quantization, runtime, prompt, and tool-schema identities;
- deterministic morning reports and quarantine queues.

## 0.3 — Forge experiment lifecycle

- micro-verifier registry;
- probe preconditions and witness schemas;
- counterfactual and mutation probe support;
- independent-probe comparison;
- verifier health and coverage records;
- skeptic-driven omitted-question discovery.

## 0.4 — verified distillation studies

- semantic clustering without source deletion;
- attribution and transfer workflows;
- success- and negative-memory ablations;
- shuffled-attribution and aggregate-only controls;
- strategy retrieval and calibration metrics;
- optional small proposer-model distillation from verified traces.

## 0.5 — MNCS Fabric execution

- authenticated worker enrollment;
- content-addressed experiment bundles;
- capability-aware dispatch;
- replicated and sharded trial matrices;
- node-loss, stale-result, duplicate, and replay handling;
- deterministic reconciliation and scaling measurements.

## 0.6 — RAVEL integration study

- RAVEL episode ingestion and candidate proposal API;
- transactional knowledge and policy candidates;
- retention, transition, planning, resource, and rollback gates;
- lineage-aware delayed credit;
- hidden transfer provider;
- equal-budget investigator and recursion controls.

## Later assurance

- hardened Fedora runner with network denial and cgroup limits;
- immutable or attested verifier nodes;
- signed ledgers and transparency log;
- external transfer and final custody;
- heterogeneous cluster studies;
- energy and compute-efficiency evidence;
- formal MNCS/MNCDS integration after eligible external evidence exists.
